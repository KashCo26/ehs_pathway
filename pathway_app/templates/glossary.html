Course Glossary & Abbreviations

Quick guide to understanding course codes, program abbreviations, credit categories, and academic term definitions.

Course Rigor & Level Codes

AP Advanced Placement

Advanced Placement

College-level courses curated by the College Board. Students can take AP exams in May to potentially earn college credit and extra GPA weighting.

HONORS Honors Level

Honors Courses

Accelerated high school curriculum designed for increased depth and analytical workload, preparing students for AP or dual enrollment coursework.

IB International Baccalaureate

International Baccalaureate

A rigorous global curriculum divided into Standard Level (SL) and Higher Level (HL) courses emphasizing critical thinking and international context.

CP College Prep

College Preparatory

Standard grade-level courses designed to meet basic high school graduation criteria and four-year university admission minimums.

Special Programs & Pathway Acronyms

PLTW Project Lead The Way

Project Lead The Way

Hands-on STEM elective tracks focusing on Engineering, Biomedical Science, and Computer Science with project-based learning.

ROP / CTE Career Tech Ed

Regional Occupational Program

Vocational and technical career-oriented courses offering real-world skills, industry certifications, and internships.

DE / CC Dual Enrollment

Dual Enrollment / Concurrent Enrollment

Courses taken through local community colleges where students earn both high school diploma credits and transferable college credits simultaneously.

TA / SA Assistant Roles

Teacher Assistant / Student Assistant

Non-academic elective credit earned by assisting staff members or school departments with administrative and operational tasks.

Requirements & UC/CSU Eligibility

A-G UC / CSU Track

A-to-G Subject Requirements

The 15 high school course requirements (A through G) mandatory for minimum freshman admission eligibility to the University of California (UC) and California State University (CSU) systems.

PE Physical Education

Physical Education

Required fitness credits. Athletic sports participation in 10th-12th grade may satisfy PE credit allocations depending on district guidelines.

VAPA Visual & Performing Arts

Visual and Performing Arts

Course category (A-G Category F) encompassing visual art, music, theater, dance, and media arts. Requires a full year sequence in a single discipline for UC eligibility.

PRE-HS Pre-High School

Middle School Accelerated Courses

Courses like Algebra I or World Languages completed in middle school. Meets prerequisite levels and A-G subject tracking, but does not count towards high school graduation credit totals.

Academic Terms & Prerequisite Conditions

PREREQ Prior Required Course

Prerequisite

A specific course or grade requirement that must be successfully completed BEFORE enrolling in a subsequent course.

COREQ Concurrent Enrollment

Corequisite

A course that must be taken at the same time (concurrently) as another linked course (e.g., AP Physics lab paired with AP Physics theory).

GPA Grade Point Average

GPA (Weighted vs Unweighted)

Unweighted GPA scales up to 4.0. Weighted GPA awards extra quality points (+1.0 point for AP/Honors/IB courses, scaling up to 5.0).

OVERRIDE Prerequisite Bypass

Course Override

An administrative bypass or user acknowledgement allowing course placement on a 4-year plan even if normal prerequisites or grade levels are unmet.
